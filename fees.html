Fees & engagement

What it costs, before you commit

Legal fees have a reputation for being quoted vaguely and revised upward. We work the other way round: a written scope and a written fee before any work starts, and fixed pricing wherever the work can honestly be defined in advance.

Our approach

Four things we commit to

01

The first 15 minutes are free

Enough to hear the facts and tell you whether you have a matter. If you do not, we will say so and you will owe nothing.

02

Written before we begin

Scope, fee, what is included and what is not — confirmed in writing and signed before the first filing.

03

Fixed where we can

Drafting, notices, title searches and uncontested filings are quoted as a single figure, not by the hour.

04

Costs disclosed separately

Court fees, stamp duty, process fees and expert charges are passed through at actuals, with receipts.
