Practice Areas

Where we can help

Six areas make up the bulk of our work. Each one starts the same way — a straight assessment of what you are facing, what it will realistically take, and what it will cost.

Family & Matrimonial

Divorce, judicial separation, maintenance, custody and guardianship — handled with discretion and a preference for settlement where it serves you.

  • Mutual consent & contested divorce
  • Maintenance and interim relief
  • Child custody and visitation
Read more

Criminal Defence

Bail, anticipatory bail, quashing and trial representation under the BNS and BNSS — including urgent, out-of-hours applications.

  • Regular & anticipatory bail
  • FIR quashing and 482 petitions
  • Trial and appellate representation
Read more

Civil & Property

Title verification, partition, tenancy, specific performance and recovery suits — plus the paperwork that prevents a dispute in the first place.

  • Title search and due diligence
  • Partition and declaration suits
  • Sale deeds, gift deeds, agreements
Read more

Motor Accident & Traffic

MACT compensation claims for injury and fatal accidents, insurance disputes, and defence in traffic and rash-driving prosecutions.

  • MACT claims and enhancement appeals
  • Insurance repudiation disputes
  • Licence and challan matters
Read more

Cheque Bounce & Recovery

Section 138 complaints under the Negotiable Instruments Act, summary suits and commercial recovery — pursued to judgment and execution.

  • Section 138 NI Act complaints
  • Demand notices and replies
  • Summary suits and execution
Read more

Consumer & Employment

Complaints before consumer commissions, service deficiency claims, and workplace matters including wrongful termination and unpaid dues.

  • District & State Commission filings
  • Builder and service-deficiency claims
  • Termination, dues and POSH matters
Read more
Why clients choose us

A practice built around being reachable

Legal problems are stressful mostly because they are opaque. We work to remove the opacity first — the rest follows more easily.

01

Free 15-minute first consultation

Tell us what happened. You will leave knowing whether you have a case, what the route looks like, and what it is likely to cost — at no charge.

02

Fees agreed in writing

Scope and fees are confirmed before we begin. No surprise line items, and no billing for a phone call that lasted four minutes.

03

You speak to your advocate

04

Updates after every hearing

A short written summary after each date — what happened, what it means, and what the next step is. In the language you prefer.
