The advocate

Who you will be dealing with

Adv. Zainab Maniyar holds an MSc alongside her LLB — a combination that shows up in how she handles evidence: methodically, and with an appetite for detail that litigation rewards.

She founded ZB Legal Solutions to run a practice on two rules. First, the client hears the realistic version of their position, not the flattering one. Second, nobody pays for work that will not move their matter forward.

Her work spans family and matrimonial disputes, criminal defence, civil and property litigation, and motor accident compensation claims — the matters that most often arrive at a neighbourhood practice, and the ones where the gap between good and indifferent representation is felt most sharply by the person living through it.

✦

How a matter usually runs

  1. First conversation, free. Fifteen minutes to understand the facts and tell you whether you have a matter worth pursuing.
  2. Written scope and fee. Before any work begins, you receive the plan and the cost in writing.
  3. Filing and conduct. Drafting, filing, and appearance — with a note to you after each hearing.
  4. Closing. A final summary of the outcome, and the documents you will want to keep.
